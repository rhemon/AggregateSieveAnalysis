# Aggregate Sieve Analysis 2 privacy policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Aggregate Sieve Analysis 2 (`io.github.rhemon.aggregatesieveanalysis.relaunch`) is a free, offline app. It does not request internet access, create accounts, display advertising, or use analytics or third-party SDKs.

You enter titles, descriptions, and sieve weights. The app stores these records in its private database on your device so you can reopen analyses and view calculations. The developer does not receive your records or share them with anyone.

If Android system backup is enabled, your device may back up and restore app data through your Google account under Google's terms. The app does not upload records itself.

You can delete individual records or all records within the app. Uninstalling removes local app data. Any system backup and its retention are controlled by your device and Google account settings.

For privacy questions, open an issue at https://github.com/rhemon/AggregateSieveAnalysis/issues.
